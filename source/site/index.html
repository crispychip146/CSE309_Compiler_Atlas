A note on the solutions

These are worked answers derived for the exam questions, not an official marking scheme. References identify the supporting course concepts. Where a paper is inconsistent or a derivation extends beyond the supplied slides, the answer says so.

Search covers the question and solution text. Use the year, section and topic filters together. Save questions for revision or mark them reviewed; those choices stay in this browser.

To save a PDF, choose Print / PDF. From a question, it prints that answer; from a catalog, it prints every answer matching your filters.
